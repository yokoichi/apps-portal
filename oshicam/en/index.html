Sharp Focus on Your Oshi and the Background

OshiCam

OshiCam is an iPhone camera app that captures photos with both a close-up subject in front, such as your oshi merch, and the background behind it in focus. It takes two photos at different focus distances in a row and merges them into one on your device.

Acrylic stands, and the scenery behind them.

  • In Manual, tap ① your oshi and then ② the background in the preview to set the focus points.
  • In Auto, OshiCam automatically finds your oshi and the background. Once the markers appear, just press the shutter to shoot.
  • It takes two shots, Near and Far, and automatically aligns and merges them.
  • All photo processing happens on your device; nothing is sent externally.

Compatible with iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
