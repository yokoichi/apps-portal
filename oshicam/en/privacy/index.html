Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

OshiCam (this “App”) is an iOS app for taking photos in which both a close-up subject, such as your oshi merch, and the distant background are in focus. This Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App never sends or collects your information — including photos, personal information, or usage data — to the developer's or any third party's servers. The App has no account registration system, and it does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

Camera Use and Image Processing

  • The App uses the camera to take photos. Using the camera requires your permission.
  • Camera footage is used only to display the preview, take photos, and analyze the scene (subject detection and depth estimation) to determine where to focus.
  • All image processing — including aligning and merging the two photos taken, subject detection, and depth estimation — happens on your device. Processing uses the Vision framework built into iOS and a machine learning model (Core ML) bundled with the App. Images are never sent externally.
  • The App does not use the microphone or location. Location data is never recorded in the photos it saves.

Photo Library Access

  • To save the photos it captures and merges, the App uses Add Photos Only access to your photo library. This permission is requested the first time you save a photo.
  • The App does not read, modify, or delete existing photos in your photo library. Photos it saves are added as new photos.
  • If you turn on “Also Save Originals” in Settings, the App also saves the Near and Far photos taken before merging, in addition to the merged photo.
  • Photos the App saves record the date and time they were taken and information identifying that they were created with the App, such as the software name “OshiCam.”

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your device. None of it is ever sent externally.

  • Settings: Aspect Ratio, whether to Show Grid, Focus Mode (Auto or Manual), and whether to also save originals.
  • Information used to decide when to show a review request: the number of times a photo has been saved successfully, and whether a review request has already been shown. Review requests are displayed using iOS's standard feature, and the App never receives the content of any review.
  • Operation logs: The App writes technical records to a file in its own private storage area, to check that camera control and image merging are working correctly. The contents are camera settings (such as focus position, lens, and zoom factor), image processing values (such as alignment correction amounts), error details, and timestamps; they never include images or personal information. The file has a maximum size, and once it is exceeded, the oldest records are deleted.
  • Temporary files for sharing: When you share a photo, the App creates a temporary file in a temporary area on your device to hand off to the destination you choose. Files in this temporary area may be automatically deleted by iOS.

Deleting Information and Changing Permissions

  • If you delete the App, the settings and operation logs it stored on your device are deleted.
  • Photos saved to your photo library remain even if you delete the App. If you want to delete them, please delete them yourself using the Photos app.
  • You can change camera and photo access at any time from the iOS Settings app.

In-App Purchase

  • The App offers a one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchase that removes the credit (the “OshiCam” text) added to photos.
  • Purchases, restores, and payment are processed by Apple's App Store. The App receives only whether a purchase has been made, through StoreKit, and never receives payment information such as credit card details.
  • Making a purchase or restoring one communicates with Apple's App Store through StoreKit.

Sharing Photos (Only When You Choose)

  • Tapping “Share” on the results screen displays the iOS share sheet (the system's standard sharing feature).
  • The merged photo is passed only to the app or service you select in the share sheet. The App never sends photos automatically.
  • Shared photos also carry the date and time they were taken and information identifying that they were created with the App (no location data is included).
  • How a photo is handled after sharing is governed by the terms of service and privacy policy of the app or service you shared it with.

Disclosure to Third Parties

The App does not provide your information to third parties. This does not apply when you choose to share a photo yourself through the share sheet (see Sharing Photos).

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include any SDK that communicates externally for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting. It does not display ads and does not track you. The only external communication the App performs is with the App Store (via StoreKit) for in-app purchases and restores (see In-App Purchase). Beyond that, the only other outbound data is what you send yourself through the share sheet (see Sharing Photos).

Changes to This Policy

This Policy may change as features are added to or changed in the App. Material changes will be announced by updating this page.
