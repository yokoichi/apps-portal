Frequently Asked Questions

How does focusing work?

You can switch between Auto and Manual above the shutter button on the camera screen.

  • Manual: In the preview, tap ① your oshi, then ② the background, and then press the shutter. A third tap, or tapping “Reset,” lets you start over.
  • Auto: Once markers appear on your oshi and the background, just press the shutter. If OshiCam has trouble choosing, tap “Set Manually” to switch to Manual.

Because the App takes two photos in a row at different focus positions, don't move your iPhone while “Capturing… Hold still” is shown. You can also review how to use the App under “How to Use” in Settings, opened from “Settings” at the top of the camera screen.

I can't save photos

Adding photos to your photo library may not be allowed. Open “Photos” for OshiCam in the iPhone Settings app and allow adding photos. You can also open OshiCam's settings from the “Open Settings” button shown when a save fails.

I want to remove the “OshiCam” credit from my photos

Merged photos taken in the free version include an “OshiCam” credit in the bottom-right corner. Buying “OshiCam Pro” (a one-time in-app purchase) removes the credit and lets you add your own signature instead: your social handle, your oshi’s name, a place, and the capture date, in the bottom-right corner or tiled across the photo. Set it up under “Signature (Pro)” in Settings. The credit on photos already saved to your photo library is not removed.

I want to restore my purchase after switching phones or reinstalling

While signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase, tap “Restore Purchase” in Settings. Restoring never charges you again.

What devices are supported?

OshiCam supports iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

How are my photos and personal information handled?

All photo processing happens on your device, and photos and personal information are never sent externally. See the Privacy Policy for details.
