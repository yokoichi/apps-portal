Frequently Asked Questions

I don't get a notification when I arrive

To be notified while the app is closed, kokomemo needs “Always” location access and permission to send notifications. In the iPhone Settings app, open kokomemo, set “Location” to “Always,” and allow “Notifications.” You can choose an alert radius of 100 m, 300 m, or 500 m. Note that the same memo won't notify you again within an hour.

How many memos can I save?

The free version lets you save up to 10 memos (memos in the Trash don't count). Buying “kokomemo Premium” (a one-time in-app purchase) unlocks unlimited memos, more categories, and any color you like for your memos and categories. All 100 icons and 12 colors are free.

I want to restore my purchase after switching phones or reinstalling

While signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase, tap “Restore Purchases” in Settings. Restoring never charges you again.

I want to recover a deleted memo

Deleted memos stay in the Trash for 30 days. You can restore them from “Trash” in Settings. After 30 days they are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

Can I sync memos with another iPhone?

Memos and photos sync between devices signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account.

What devices are supported?

kokomemo supports iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How are my memos and location handled?

Your memos, photos, and locations are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud, and they are never sent to the developer's or any third party's servers. See the Privacy Policy for details.
