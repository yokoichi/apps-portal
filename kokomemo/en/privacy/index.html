Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 / Last updated: November 26, 2026

kokomemo (this “App”) is an iOS app that links memos to places and notifies you when you arrive at one of them. This Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

The developer does not operate any server for the App. The App never sends or collects your information — including memos, location, photos, or usage data — to the developer's or any third party's servers. The App has no account registration system, and it does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

Where Your Data Is Stored and iCloud Sync

The following information you create in the App is stored and synced only on your device and in your own iCloud (the private database of Apple's CloudKit). Information stored in iCloud belongs to your Apple Account, and the developer cannot access its contents.

  • Memos: the text and checklists.
  • Places: coordinates, place names, prefectures, and cities.
  • Category, color, and icon settings.
  • Photos attached to memos.

When iCloud is unavailable (for example, when you are not signed in to iCloud), this information is stored only on your device.

Information Stored Only on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your device. It is never sent externally and is not synced to iCloud.

  • Place search history: the names and coordinates of places you chose from search results (up to the 10 most recent).
  • Display settings: how the list is grouped and sorted, map display, appearance, and similar settings.
  • Content saved from the share sheet: the text and, if available, the coordinates of your current location when you saved it. It is kept temporarily in a storage area on your device reserved for the App (an App Group) only until the App turns it into a memo, and is deleted afterward. Content that has not been imported after 7 days is also deleted. Once imported, the memo is stored on your device and in iCloud like any other memo.

Use of Location

  • The App uses your location to show where you are on the map, to choose places for your memos, and to detect when you arrive at a saved place. Using location requires your permission.
  • Arrival detection uses iOS region monitoring. To notify you even when the App is closed, the App asks you to allow location access “Always.”
  • iOS can monitor up to 20 places at a time, so when you have many places, the App switches the monitored places to those nearest your current location. To do this, it uses iOS significant location change notifications.
  • When you save a memo from the share sheet, the App gets your current location once and uses it as the memo's place. The share sheet never asks for location permission, and if location access isn't allowed for the App, no location is used.
  • The App does not record a history of your location, and it never sends your location to the developer.

Use of Apple's Map Services

To search for places and to look up place names, prefectures, and cities, the App uses Apple's map services (MapKit place search and Core Location geocoding). When it does, the search terms you enter and the coordinates of the place you choose are sent to Apple. Apple's handling of this information is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Adding Photos

  • The App imports only the photos you choose from your photo library. It uses the iOS photo picker and does not ask for access to your entire photo library.
  • Imported photos are resized and saved as JPEG. Metadata in the original photo, such as the location where it was taken, is not saved.

Notifications

Arrival notifications are local notifications created on your device. The App has no server for sending push notifications. Notifications require your permission.

In-App Purchase

  • The App offers a one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchase, “kokomemo Premium.”
  • Purchases, restores, and payment are processed by Apple's App Store. The App receives only whether a purchase has been made, through StoreKit, and never receives payment information such as credit card details.
  • Making a purchase or restoring one communicates with Apple's App Store through StoreKit.

Disclosure to Third Parties

The App does not provide your information to third parties. Communication with the Apple services the App uses (iCloud, map services, and the App Store) is described in Where Your Data Is Stored and iCloud Sync, Use of Apple's Map Services, and In-App Purchase.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The App does not include any SDK for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting. It does not display ads and does not track you.

Deleting Information and Changing Permissions

  • When you delete a memo, it moves to the Trash and is permanently deleted after 30 days. You can also delete it permanently from the Trash right away.
  • If you delete the App, the information it stored on your device is deleted.
  • You can delete information stored in iCloud yourself from iCloud storage management in the iOS Settings app.
  • You can change location and notification permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Children

The App has no features designed for children or for any specific age group.
