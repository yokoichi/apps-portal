Memos that pop up on arrival

kokomemo

kokomemo is an iPhone app that lets you pin a memo to any place on the map and notifies you when you get there. Drop “milk, eggs, plastic wrap” on the supermarket, or “buy the local sweets” on a sightseeing spot, and your memo pops up the moment you arrive, even when the app is closed.

App Store submission in preparation

Pin a memo to a place. Get notified on arrival.

  • Tap the map, search for a place, or use your current location to choose where your memo goes.
  • Choose an alert radius of 100 m, 300 m, or 500 m. When you arrive, you get a notification even if the app isn't running.
  • The same memo won't notify you again within an hour.
  • More than 20 places? kokomemo keeps the nearest ones active automatically. It uses iOS region monitoring instead of constant GPS, so it is battery-friendly.

Built for real life

  • Add checklists to your memos and check items off right from the memo.
  • Attach up to 5 photos to a memo.
  • Organize memos with categories, colors, and icons. All 100 icons and 12 colors are free.
  • Group the list by category, area (prefecture), or distance, and sort it by nearest, newest, or address. You can also filter by area.
  • Deleted memos stay in the Trash for 30 days, so you can restore them.
  • Memos and photos sync across your devices with iCloud.

Free and Premium

  • Free: up to 10 memos (memos in the Trash don't count), all 100 icons, 12 colors, and the Shopping category.
  • kokomemo Premium (a one-time purchase, not a subscription): unlimited memos, more categories (Food, Work, Home, Travel, Health, Leisure, and Other, plus categories you make yourself), and any color you like for your memos and categories.
  • Family Sharing is supported.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your memos, photos, and locations are stored only on your device and in your own iCloud. kokomemo asks for “Always” location access so it can notify you when the app is closed.

Compatible with iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
