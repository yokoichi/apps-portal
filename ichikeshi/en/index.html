Ichikeshi: Photo GPS Remover

Ichikeshi is an iPhone app that removes location and other metadata from copies of your photos, so you can save, share, or copy them. Your original photos stay unchanged, and all processing happens on your device.

Hand over a photo, not where you took it.

  • Choose a photo to create a copy with location and other metadata removed, then save, share, or copy it. Your original photo stays as it is.
  • Find photos in your library that have location data, right on your device.
  • Do the same from the share sheet in Photos and other apps.
  • Live Photos keep their motion and sound.
  • The free version removes location data. Premium (a one-time purchase) adds “All but Date,” “All Metadata,” and “Custom.”
  • Your photos and location data are never sent externally.

Compatible with iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
