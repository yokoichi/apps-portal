Ichikeshi

Remove location data from a photo copy.

Select photos, remove metadata from a copy, then save or share it. Your original photo stays unchanged.

Get it on the App Store

Compatible with iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Check photo information before sharing.

  • The free version removes GPS location data from a copy.
  • A one-time Premium purchase adds “All but Date,” “All Metadata,” and “Custom” removal options.
  • Find photos with location data, or process photos from the share sheet.
  • Live Photos retain their motion and audio.

Local photo processing, with separate ad disclosures.

Photos are processed on your device. Photos, embedded GPS data and photo metadata are not sent to the operator or ad services. In the free version, AdMob and UMP may collect device information, IP addresses and other data for advertising and consent management. This does not remove visible landmarks or places from the image. Sharing destinations and OS services follow their own settings and policies.
