Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Ichikeshi: Photo GPS Remover (this “App”) is an iOS app that removes location and other metadata from copies of your photos so you can save, share, or copy them. This Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Information We Collect

All photo processing happens on your device. The operator of the App runs no servers, and your photos, location data, and metadata are never sent to the operator or any third party. The App has no account registration system, and it does not collect personal information such as your name or email address.

However, the free version displays ads, and Google AdMob may collect information for that purpose (see Advertising).

Photo Processing

  • The App creates a copy of the photo you choose and removes location and other metadata from the copy. Your original photo is never modified.
  • In the free version, the App removes location data only. Premium also offers “All but Date,” “All Metadata,” and “Custom.”
  • Live Photos are processed with their motion and sound kept intact.
  • At your direction, the processed copy is saved to your photo library, shared, or copied to the clipboard.

Photo Library Access

  • The App uses the standard iOS photo picker to let you choose photos. Choosing photos with the photo picker does not require photo library access.
  • Photo library access is used only for the following purposes:
    • Finding photos with location data: the App checks on your device whether photos in your library have location data.
    • Saving copies: the App saves processed copies to its own album in your photo library.
    • Deleting originals: only when you ask it to, the App deletes the original photo. Deletion happens after iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos move to Recently Deleted.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your device. None of it is ever sent to the operator.

  • Processing history: only the date and time, the number of photos, the source (Photos, Files, Search, or the share sheet), and the removal setting. Photos and location data are never stored in the history. You can turn processing history on or off in Settings (it is on by default) and clear it from Settings.
  • Information used to verify purchases: the product ID, transaction ID, and the date and time of verification, stored to check your purchase status.

Clipboard

When you copy a processed photo to the clipboard, it is copied for use on this device only and is set not to sync to the clipboard on your other devices.

In-App Purchase

  • The App offers a one-time in-app purchase, “Premium.”
  • Purchases, restores, and payment are processed by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). The operator never receives payment information such as credit card details.

Advertising

  • The free version displays ads from Google AdMob, an ad service provided by Google LLC.
  • The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent for ads.
  • To deliver ads, AdMob may collect information such as information about your device, your IP address, and ad impressions and taps. For how Google handles this information, see Google's policy (opens in a new tab).
  • The App does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • With Premium, the App shows no ads and does not load ads.
  • The share sheet feature (share extension) shows no ads.

Disclosure to Third Parties

The App does not provide your photos, location data, or metadata to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • In the free version, when Google AdMob collects information to deliver ads (see Advertising).
  • When you choose to share a photo yourself through the share sheet. How a photo is handled after sharing is governed by the terms of service and privacy policy of the app or service you shared it with.

Analytics and Tracking

The App does not include its own analytics SDK.

Deleting Information and Changing Permissions

  • You can clear processing history from the App's Settings.
  • Copies saved to your photo library remain even if you delete the App. If you want to delete them, please delete them yourself using the Photos app.
  • You can change photo access at any time from the iOS Settings app.

Changes to This Policy

This Policy may change as features are added to or changed in the App. Material changes will be announced by updating this page.
