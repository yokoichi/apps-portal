Yorishiro

Yorishiro is a journaling app for self-understanding. As you write about everyday events and feelings, AI gently organizes your thinking patterns and emotional flow.

Every entry brings you closer to yourself.

The more you write, the more clearly you begin to see yourself. Yorishiro turns ordinary daily entries into lasting clues for understanding who you are.

In old Japan, a yorishiro was a vessel in which the unseen could dwell. This app is a modern-day vessel where the person you are gradually takes shape through your own words.
