Because this is a digital service, returns and cancellations for customer convenience are not accepted after purchase.

iOS version: Refund eligibility is governed by Apple's refund policy. Please contact Apple directly to request a refund.

Web version: Refunds for customer convenience are not accepted after service begins, except where required by law. Canceling a recurring subscription

Purchases made through the iOS version: Cancel through the App Store subscription management screen. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Purchases made through the web version: Sign in at https://yorishiro.me, then go to Settings → Plans & Billing → Manage Subscription. Paid features remain available until the end of the current billing period after cancellation.
